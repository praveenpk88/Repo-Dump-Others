# Instagram launch post

A 5-slide carousel (1080×1350, Instagram's 4:5 portrait size) to open the pizzeria's new account, in the il Mercato look.

1. **Ciao Melbourne!** hook slide with the hero pizza (also works as a single-image post)
2. **Pick your level**: Classic → Level up → Go all in, shown with Margherita / Bufalina / Prosciutto
3. **The dough comes first**: close-up of the crust and Samuele's flour and hydration line
4. **What's your pick?** nine pizzas, asking for comments
5. **Follow for more** end card (also usable as the last frame of a Reel)

`caption.txt` has the post caption and hashtags. Rebuild with `node build.js`; it reuses fonts from `../halloween-poster/fonts`,
photos from `../pizza-menu/photos` and `../halloween-poster/photos`, and the logo from `../pizza-menu/brand`.
