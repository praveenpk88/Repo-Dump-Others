Napoletana

by Samuele Ferro
At the heart of every pizza is Samuele’s passion: the dough. Combining elements of traditional Napoletana and contemporanea pizza-making, he continually experiments with flour, fermentation and hydration to create his perfect dough.Curious? Ask Samuele what flour and hydration he’s using today.

How to read the menu

  1. 1Classic: the flavour as Naples makes it.
  2. 2Level up: the same pizza with a twist.
  3. 3Go all in: loaded with more on top.

Vegan / can be made vegan

Ask our team and we’ll make it happen.

GF bases available

Our gluten-free dough is made in-house by Samuele, who holds a Coeliac Australia certificate. All pizzas are cooked in the same oven as our regular bases, so we cannot guarantee the absence of gluten or cross-contact.
